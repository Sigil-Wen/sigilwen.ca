About me

I’m a self-taught engineer, 2025 Thiel Fellow, Magnificent Grant recipient, a16z New Media Fellow, M&T at UPenn opt-out, and O-1 visa holder.

Instead of going to college, I spent my formative years hacking at the heart of AI. For 11 months, I lived in a hacker house alongside builders like Andrej Karpathy. It was a magical time: I was 18 and spent weekends hacking with the founders of Anthropic, Perplexity, Replicate, and RunPod, as well as the researchers behind DALL·E, Whisper, GPT-3, and Stable Diffusion.

I love local models & the exhilarating exponential of the intelligence Pareto frontier. In 2019 I started by running YOLO object detection on my desktop GPU. In 2023 I got GPT-2 running on my Apple Watch. When whisper was open sourced, at Airchat, we were the first to productionize it onto the iPhone. I recently got Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter model with Opus 4.8-level performance, running at six tokens per second across two 512 GB M3 Ultra Mac Studios connected over Thunderbolt. The future is exciting.

I received my EB-1A Extraordinary Ability green card at 20 and became the cover image on Google for “O-1 visa” after creating the #1 video on the internet. I’m the guy behind the infamous Alien of Extraordinary Ability hoodies.

After getting millions of views helping extraordinary talent, I naturally founded Extraordinary, which now powers top technology companies like Cognition, Lovable, Etched, Zapier, a16z, Founders Fund, and hundreds more. As CEO, I grew the company to seven-figure profitability in its first year while helping some of the most talented people come to America. I’ve since transitioned to Executive Chairman, and I couldn’t be prouder of our ever-growing team.

I’m interested in crypto, distributed systems, AI, economics, and incentives. I spend my time reading, programming, and trying to think for myself. I love working very hard.

I believe AI will allow the most capable individuals to execute 1,000× faster—fast enough to outpace entire companies. My goal is to master this new age of infinite leverage and use it to create great work.

To me, truly loving yourself means recognizing that you’re capable of making a significant positive dent in the universe.

I’m the author of Web 4.0, which reached 10 million views in 48 hours. I’m now building Conway.
